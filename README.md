## 🚀 Featured Project — FuelDrop

> **Find the cheapest petrol near you — on your phone or on your drive.**

![iOS 17+](https://img.shields.io/badge/iOS-17%2B-000000?style=flat-square&logo=apple&logoColor=white)
![CarPlay](https://img.shields.io/badge/CarPlay-Supported-1C1C1E?style=flat-square&logo=apple&logoColor=white)
![SwiftUI](https://img.shields.io/badge/SwiftUI-F05138?style=flat-square&logo=swift&logoColor=white)
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-In%20Development-F5A623?style=flat-square)

FuelDrop helps UK drivers find the cheapest nearby petrol station in seconds — with full **CarPlay support** so you never have to look down while driving.

### ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 📍 **Nearby Stations** | Sorted by price and distance, updated live |
| 🚗 **CarPlay Integration** | Glanceable prices before you pull in |
| 🔲 **Home & Lock Screen Widgets** | Your cheapest station at a glance |
| 🎨 **Scandinavian-inspired UI** | Clean, focused design built in SwiftUI |
| 🚗 **Volvo connected car services Integration** | See the cost of filling your tank including your trip to the station based on live data from your Volvo |
